## Format & Resolution Guidelines
- **Preferred format:** WebP or PNG.
- **Stories & Banners:** 16:9 or 16:10 ratio (1920x1080 or 1600x1000).
- **Architecture & Pantheon Cards:** 4:3 or 1:1 ratio (1200x900 or 1000x1000).
- **File size:** Aim for under 300KB per image for optimal loading speed across mobile connections in Uganda and East Africa.
